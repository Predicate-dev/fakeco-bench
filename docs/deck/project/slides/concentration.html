We pooled every scored chat: 7,304 of them. Chats that need a single decision, such as a refund window, a cap, who owns the account, an ambiguous request or a forged memo, fail 2.1% of the time. Chats that pick up a colleague's note fail 12%. Chats that finish a batch someone else started fail 54%. For the three strongest models the same three numbers are 0.3%, 1.8% and 8.8%.
