We started with an ordinary tool-use benchmark. An agent works a support shift at a simulated company: eight to fourteen chats, with tools that move real balances in a ledger. Chat by chat, the models look finished. Claude Opus 5.5 gets 96% of chats right. It passes 58% of shifts. Sonnet 5.5 gets 94% of chats right and passes 29% of shifts. Sonnet 4.6 gets 82% of chats right and passes none. So the question was: where do the missing chats go? Are they spread evenly, or is it one thing?
