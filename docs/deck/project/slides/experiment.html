So is this something the models cannot do, or something they do not think to do? We added one line to GPT-6.1 Sol's instructions: when you work through a list, group it by customer before you issue anything. Same twelve shifts, same grading. It went from 7 of 12 to 12 of 12, and from twenty repeated customers paid twice to none. So the model can keep the rule. It does not look for it unless told. One model, one run each, and the line names what to group by, so this shows the ability is there, not that a general reminder would work.
